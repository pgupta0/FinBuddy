# Glossary of Financial Terms for First-Time Indian Investors (English–Hindi)

This glossary explains common mutual fund and personal-finance terms in plain English, with the
equivalent Hindi term (in Devanagari) and a short Hindi explanation. It is written for someone who
has opened a savings or investment account but has no formal finance training. Terms are grouped by
theme; each entry stands on its own so it can be looked up individually.

Sources used to check definitions: [AMFI Investor Awareness — Mutual Funds Sahi Hai](https://www.mutualfundssahihai.com/), [AMFI India — Systematic Investment Plan](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/become-mf-distributor?zoneName=sip), [SEBI Investor Website](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/).

## Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) — व्यवस्थित निवेश योजना (SIP)

A SIP is a way of investing a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals (usually monthly)
instead of investing a large sum all at once. The bank auto-debits the chosen amount on a fixed date
and it buys units of the fund at that day's price (NAV). Because the purchase price varies over time,
a SIP averages out the cost of buying across market ups and downs — this effect is often called
"rupee cost averaging."

हिंदी में: SIP का मतलब है हर महीने एक तय तारीख को एक निश्चित रकम म्यूचुअल फंड में अपने-आप निवेश हो
जाना, बजाय एक साथ पूरी रकम लगाने के। इससे बाजार के ऊपर-नीचे होने पर भी औसत खरीद कीमत संतुलित रहती है।

## Lump Sum Investment — एकमुश्त निवेश

Investing the entire amount at one time, in one transaction, rather than spreading it out. Suitable
when a large sum becomes available at once (a bonus, maturity proceeds) and the investor has already
decided on an allocation strategy, since timing a single large investment carries more short-term
market-timing risk than a SIP.

हिंदी में: एकमुश्त निवेश का मतलब है पूरी रकम एक ही बार में निवेश करना, किश्तों में नहीं।

## Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) — व्यवस्थित स्थानांतरण योजना (STP)

A standing instruction to automatically move a fixed amount, at regular intervals, from one mutual
fund scheme to another within the same fund house — commonly used to gradually move a lump sum from
a low-risk debt fund into an equity fund over several months rather than all at once.

हिंदी में: STP के ज़रिए एक तय रकम, तय समय पर, एक फंड से दूसरे फंड में अपने-आप ट्रांसफर होती रहती है —
जैसे एकमुश्त रकम को धीरे-धीरे डेट फंड से इक्विटी फंड में ले जाना।

## Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) — व्यवस्थित निकासी योजना (SWP)

The reverse of a SIP: a standing instruction to redeem (sell) a fixed amount from a mutual fund
investment at regular intervals and pay it out to the investor — used to create a regular income
stream from an accumulated corpus, for example after retirement.

हिंदी में: SWP में निवेशक हर महीने एक तय रकम अपने म्यूचुअल फंड निवेश से निकालता है, जैसे नियमित आय का
एक जरिया।

## Net Asset Value (NAV) — शुद्ध परिसंपत्ति मूल्य (NAV)

The price of one unit of a mutual fund scheme on a given day, calculated as the total value of
everything the scheme owns (its investments plus cash), minus its liabilities, divided by the number
of units outstanding. Mutual funds are bought and sold at their NAV, not at a stock-exchange-style
live price (except for ETFs, which do trade throughout the day like a stock).

हिंदी में: NAV म्यूचुअल फंड की एक यूनिट की उस दिन की कीमत होती है — फंड की कुल संपत्ति में से देनदारियां
घटाकर, कुल यूनिट्स से भाग देने पर मिलती है।

## Total Expense Ratio (TER) / Expense Ratio — कुल व्यय अनुपात

The annual fee a mutual fund charges its investors, expressed as a percentage of the money invested,
to cover the fund's management, administration, and distribution costs. It is deducted from the
fund's returns automatically — an investor never receives a separate bill for it, but it directly
reduces the return actually received. SEBI caps the maximum expense ratio a scheme can charge, and
the cap decreases in slabs as the scheme's total assets under management (AUM) grow, so that
economies of scale get passed back to investors. As illustrative slabs (SEBI can revise these; always
check the specific scheme's current factsheet for the exact number in effect): equity-oriented
schemes are typically capped around 2.10% per year on the first ₹500 crore of assets, stepping down
through several slabs to roughly 1% or lower once a scheme is very large; debt-oriented schemes have
similar, slightly lower slabs. See [Kotak Mutual Fund — What is Expense Ratio](https://www.kotakmf.com/Information/blogs/what-is-expense-ratio-in-mutual-fund) for the full slab table.

हिंदी में: एक्सपेंस रेशियो वह सालाना फीस है जो म्यूचुअल फंड कंपनी फंड चलाने के लिए काटती है। यह सीधे
आपके रिटर्न में से घटती है और SEBI ने इसकी अधिकतम सीमा तय कर रखी है, जो फंड का आकार बढ़ने पर घटती जाती
है।

## Direct Plan vs. Regular Plan — डायरेक्ट प्लान बनाम रेगुलर प्लान

Every mutual fund scheme is sold in two versions with the same underlying investments but different
costs. A **Regular Plan** is bought through a distributor or advisor, and its expense ratio includes a
commission paid to that distributor. A **Direct Plan** is bought straight from the fund house (through
its website, app, or a registered investment adviser charging a separate fee), has no distributor
commission built in, and therefore carries a lower expense ratio — typically 0.5 to 1.5 percentage
points lower per year, though the exact gap varies by scheme and fund house and should be checked on
the scheme's current factsheet rather than assumed. Over many years, this difference compounds into a
meaningfully different final amount for the same underlying investments.

हिंदी में: हर म्यूचुअल फंड स्कीम दो रूपों में बिकती है — रेगुलर प्लान (डिस्ट्रीब्यूटर के ज़रिए, जिसमें
कमीशन शामिल है) और डायरेक्ट प्लान (सीधे फंड कंपनी से, बिना कमीशन के, इसलिए फीस कम)। लंबे समय में यह
फर्क बड़ा असर डालता है।

## Exit Load — एग्ज़िट लोड

A fee charged if an investor redeems (sells) units before a specified minimum holding period —
commonly around 1% if redeemed within one year for many equity schemes, though the exact period and
percentage vary by scheme and must be checked on that scheme's factsheet. It exists to discourage
very short-term, speculative use of a fund designed for longer holding periods.

हिंदी में: एग्ज़िट लोड वह शुल्क है जो तय समय से पहले यूनिट बेचने पर लगता है, ताकि निवेशक जल्दबाज़ी में
पैसा न निकालें।

## Folio Number — फोलियो नंबर

A unique account number assigned to an investor by a mutual fund's registrar the first time they
invest in that fund house — similar to a bank account number. All future investments and redemptions
with that same fund house, across different schemes, can be linked under the same folio.

हिंदी में: फोलियो नंबर आपका उस फंड कंपनी के साथ खाता नंबर है, जैसे बैंक अकाउंट नंबर — हर बार निवेश करते
समय इसी फोलियो से जोड़ा जा सकता है।

## Asset Allocation — एसेट एलोकेशन (परिसंपत्ति आवंटन)

The decision of how to split money across broad categories of investments — chiefly equity (shares),
debt (fixed income), gold, and cash — based on an investor's goals, time horizon, and comfort with
ups and downs in value. Research on institutional and individual portfolios consistently finds that
this top-level split, more than the choice of any single fund or stock, is the biggest driver of a
portfolio's long-term risk and return characteristics.

हिंदी में: एसेट एलोकेशन यानी अपने पैसे को इक्विटी, डेट, सोना और नकद जैसी अलग-अलग श्रेणियों में बांटने
का फैसला — यही तय करता है कि आपका पोर्टफोलियो कितना जोखिम भरा या स्थिर रहेगा।

## Asset Class — एसेट क्लास

A broad category of investment that shares similar characteristics and behaves in a broadly similar
way — for example equity (ownership in companies), debt/fixed income (loans that pay interest), gold,
real estate, and cash equivalents. Different asset classes tend to rise and fall at different times,
which is the basis for diversification.

हिंदी में: एसेट क्लास निवेश की एक बड़ी श्रेणी है — जैसे इक्विटी, डेट, सोना, रियल एस्टेट — जो एक जैसे
व्यवहार करती है।

## Diversification — विविधीकरण

Spreading money across many different investments (different companies, sectors, asset classes)
instead of concentrating it in one or a few, so that poor performance in any single investment has a
limited effect on the overall portfolio. A mutual fund itself is a diversification tool, since one
unit represents a small slice of many underlying holdings.

हिंदी में: विविधीकरण का मतलब है पैसे को कई अलग-अलग जगह लगाना, ताकि किसी एक निवेश के खराब प्रदर्शन का
पूरे पोर्टफोलियो पर बहुत असर न पड़े।

## Rebalancing — रीबैलेंसिंग

Periodically adjusting a portfolio back toward its originally intended asset allocation, because
different asset classes grow at different rates over time and drift the actual mix away from the
target — for example, if equities rise faster than debt, the portfolio can become more equity-heavy
and riskier than intended unless some equity gains are periodically moved back into debt.

हिंदी में: रीबैलेंसिंग यानी समय-समय पर पोर्टफोलियो को वापस उसके तय किए गए अनुपात में लाना, क्योंकि
अलग-अलग एसेट अलग गति से बढ़ते हैं।

## Riskometer — रिस्कोमीटर

A standardized, SEBI-mandated visual gauge that every mutual fund scheme must display, showing the
scheme's risk level on a six-point scale: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High, and
Very High. Every fund house uses the same scale and the same methodology, so riskometers are directly
comparable across schemes and fund houses. See [SEBI Investor Website — Riskometer](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/riskometer.html).

हिंदी में: रिस्कोमीटर एक तयशुदा पैमाना है जो हर स्कीम को छह स्तरों में से एक जोखिम स्तर बताता है — कम,
कम से मध्यम, मध्यम, मध्यम से अधिक, अधिक, बहुत अधिक — और यह हर फंड कंपनी में एक जैसा होता है।

## Compounding — चक्रवृद्धि

The process by which investment returns themselves start earning further returns over time, so that
growth accelerates the longer money stays invested. This is why starting to invest early, even with
small amounts, tends to matter more than the exact amount invested later.

हिंदी में: चक्रवृद्धि का मतलब है कि रिटर्न पर भी रिटर्न मिलना शुरू हो जाता है, जिससे समय के साथ बढ़त
तेज़ होती जाती है — इसीलिए जल्दी निवेश शुरू करना फायदेमंद है।

## CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) — चक्रवृद्धि वार्षिक वृद्धि दर

A single annualized percentage figure that describes how an investment grew, on average, each year
over a given period, smoothing out year-to-year ups and downs into one comparable number. It is
useful for comparing the historical performance of different funds or strategies over the same time
period, though past CAGR does not guarantee similar returns in the future.

हिंदी में: CAGR एक ऐसा वार्षिक प्रतिशत है जो बताता है कि किसी निवेश ने औसतन हर साल कितना बढ़त दिखाई,
अलग-अलग सालों के उतार-चढ़ाव को मिलाकर।

## XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return) — XIRR

An annualized return figure similar to CAGR, but designed for investments made at irregular dates and
amounts — such as a SIP, where money is invested on different dates rather than as one lump sum. XIRR
is the standard way SIP returns are reported, since a simple CAGR calculation does not account for
multiple cash flows on different dates.

हिंदी में: XIRR भी सालाना रिटर्न बताता है, लेकिन यह तब इस्तेमाल होता है जब निवेश अलग-अलग तारीखों और
रकम में हुआ हो — जैसे SIP में।

## Volatility — उतार-चढ़ाव (वोलैटिलिटी)

How much and how quickly the value of an investment moves up and down over a given period. Higher
volatility means larger swings (both gains and losses) in the short term; equity investments are
generally more volatile than debt investments, especially over short holding periods.

हिंदी में: वोलैटिलिटी यानी किसी निवेश की कीमत कितनी तेज़ी से ऊपर-नीचे होती है — इक्विटी में यह आमतौर पर
डेट से ज़्यादा होती है, खासकर कम समय में।

## Drawdown — गिरावट (ड्रॉडाउन)

The percentage decline in the value of an investment from its most recent peak to its lowest point
before it recovers. It is one way to describe how much value an investor might have seen erode during
a difficult market period, which is different from — and often larger than — the eventual net loss or
gain over the full holding period.

हिंदी में: ड्रॉडाउन यानी किसी निवेश की कीमत अपने सबसे ऊंचे स्तर से कितनी नीचे गिरी, ठीक होने से पहले।

## Liquidity — तरलता (लिक्विडिटी)

How quickly and easily an investment can be converted back into cash without a significant loss of
value. Most open-ended mutual funds are highly liquid (redeemed within a few working days); real
estate and some fixed deposits with lock-in periods are comparatively illiquid.

हिंदी में: लिक्विडिटी यानी किसी निवेश को कितनी आसानी और जल्दी नकद में बदला जा सकता है, बिना कीमत में
बड़ा नुकसान हुए।

## Inflation — मुद्रास्फीति (महंगाई)

The rate at which the general price level of goods and services rises over time, which erodes the
purchasing power of money that is not invested or is invested at a rate below inflation. A key reason
long-term savings are usually invested rather than held only as cash is to try to grow faster than
inflation.

हिंदी में: महंगाई यानी समय के साथ चीज़ों के दाम बढ़ना, जिससे बिना निवेश किए रखे पैसे की खरीद क्षमता कम
होती जाती है।

## Risk Profile / Risk Appetite — जोखिम प्रोफ़ाइल

A description of how much investment risk (potential for short-term loss in exchange for potentially
higher long-term return) a particular investor can and is willing to take, based on their financial
capacity to absorb a loss, their emotional comfort with volatility, and the return they need to meet
their goals. These three factors do not always point the same way, which is why a good risk
assessment considers them separately rather than blending them into a single number.

हिंदी में: रिस्क प्रोफाइल यह बताता है कि कोई निवेशक कितना जोखिम उठा सकता है और उठाना चाहता है — यह उसकी
आर्थिक क्षमता, मानसिक सहजता और लक्ष्य के लिए ज़रूरी रिटर्न, तीनों पर निर्भर करता है।

## Benchmark — बेंचमार्क

A standard index (such as the Nifty 50 or Sensex for large-cap equity funds) that a mutual fund's
performance is compared against, to judge whether the fund's managers added value relative to simply
holding the broad market.

हिंदी में: बेंचमार्क एक तय इंडेक्स है (जैसे निफ्टी 50 या सेंसेक्स) जिससे किसी फंड के प्रदर्शन की तुलना
की जाती है।

## Know Your Customer (KYC) — केवाईसी

A mandatory identity- and address-verification process that every investor in India must complete
once (using PAN, Aadhaar, and other documents) before investing in mutual funds or other securities
market products, as required by SEBI to prevent fraud and money laundering.

हिंदी में: केवाईसी एक अनिवार्य पहचान-सत्यापन प्रक्रिया है, जो हर निवेशक को म्यूचुअल फंड या शेयर बाजार
में निवेश से पहले एक बार पूरी करनी होती है।

## Asset Management Company (AMC) — एसेट मैनेजमेंट कंपनी

The company that actually manages a mutual fund scheme — pooling investor money, deciding what to buy
and sell within the scheme's stated objective, and charging the expense ratio for doing so. Examples
include the major fund houses whose scheme names appear on platforms and apps.

हिंदी में: एसेट मैनेजमेंट कंपनी वह कंपनी है जो म्यूचुअल फंड स्कीम को असल में चलाती है — निवेशकों का
पैसा जोड़कर उसे कहां लगाना है, यह तय करती है।

## AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India) — एएमएफआई

The industry body for all SEBI-registered mutual fund houses in India. AMFI runs investor-education
programs (including the "Mutual Funds Sahi Hai" campaign), maintains registration for mutual fund
distributors, and publishes daily NAV data across the industry. See [amfiindia.com](https://www.amfiindia.com/).

हिंदी में: एएमएफआई भारत में सभी SEBI-पंजीकृत म्यूचुअल फंड कंपनियों की संस्था है, जो निवेशक जागरूकता और
डेटा प्रकाशन का काम करती है।

## Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) — ईएलएसएस

A category of equity mutual fund that qualifies for a tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income
Tax Act, up to the section's overall limit, in exchange for a mandatory three-year lock-in period —
the shortest lock-in among all Section 80C tax-saving instruments.

हिंदी में: ईएलएसएस एक इक्विटी फंड है जिसमें निवेश पर धारा 80C के तहत टैक्स छूट मिलती है, लेकिन इसमें
तीन साल का लॉक-इन होता है।

## Index Fund — इंडेक्स फंड

A mutual fund that simply buys and holds all (or a representative sample) of the securities in a
specific market index, such as the Nifty 50, in the same proportion as the index — aiming to match the
index's return rather than to beat it. Because there is no active stock-picking, index funds
typically have a lower expense ratio than actively managed funds.

हिंदी में: इंडेक्स फंड किसी तय इंडेक्स (जैसे निफ्टी 50) के सभी शेयर उसी अनुपात में खरीदता है, ताकि उसका
रिटर्न इंडेक्स जैसा ही रहे — इसकी फीस आमतौर पर कम होती है।

## Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF) — एक्सचेंज ट्रेडेड फंड (ईटीएफ)

Similar to an index fund in that it tracks an index, but an ETF trades on a stock exchange throughout
the day at a live market price (like a share), whereas a regular mutual fund is bought and sold only
once a day at its end-of-day NAV. Buying an ETF requires a demat and trading account.

हिंदी में: ईटीएफ भी किसी इंडेक्स को ट्रैक करता है, लेकिन यह शेयर की तरह पूरे दिन एक्सचेंज पर खरीदा-बेचा
जा सकता है — इसके लिए डीमैट खाता ज़रूरी है।

## Arbitrage Fund — आर्बिट्राज फंड

A hybrid mutual fund that profits from small, low-risk price differences for the same security
between the cash market and the futures market, rather than betting on the market's overall
direction. It is taxed like an equity fund but tends to behave more like a low-risk debt investment in
practice.

हिंदी में: आर्बिट्राज फंड एक ही शेयर की कैश और फ्यूचर्स कीमत के छोटे अंतर से कम-जोखिम वाला मुनाफा
कमाता है — टैक्स के लिहाज़ से इसे इक्विटी फंड माना जाता है।

## Fund of Funds (FoF) — फंड ऑफ फंड्स

A mutual fund that, instead of directly holding stocks or bonds, invests in units of other mutual
funds (which may include funds from other fund houses, or international funds), giving exposure to
those underlying funds' strategies through a single scheme.
